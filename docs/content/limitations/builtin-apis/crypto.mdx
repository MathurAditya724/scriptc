- **Crypto:** Hashing, HMAC, PBKDF2, and HKDF support every digest `crypto.getHashes()` lists in Node 24, with OpenSSL's aliases and object identifiers. Hash and Hmac accept every Buffer encoding and any TypedArray or DataView, `createHash` and `hash.copy` take `outputLength`, and `crypto.hash` takes its options object; HMAC, PBKDF2, and HKDF reject the SHAKE and KMAC digests with Node's errors. `timingSafeEqual` compares any view or ArrayBuffer. Secret KeyObjects (`createSecretKey`, `generateKey` and `generateKeySync` for `hmac` and `aes`) support `type`, `symmetricKeySize`, `export()`, `equals()`, and `instanceof KeyObject`, and createHmac and hkdf accept them; asymmetric keys, `KeyObject.from()`, and `toCryptoKey()` are not available. `randomFill`, `randomFillSync` (every view kind and ArrayBuffer), `randomInt`, `randomUUID`, and `randomUUIDv7` follow Node 24.22, including callback timing; a `randomInt` callback whose error parameter is typed `Error | null` receives `null` where Node passes `undefined`. Scrypt supports its default parameters, numeric options, aliases, and memory limit. Both KDFs accept strings, Buffers, numeric typed arrays, DataViews, and fixed-length ArrayBuffers. Cipher or public-key operations remain unsupported. Native derivations finish before their deferred callbacks, so computation blocks the caller.
