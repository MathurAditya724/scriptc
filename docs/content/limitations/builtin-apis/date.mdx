- **Date:** Spread constructor arguments, setters, thrown Date values, and locale formatters are unsupported.
  Dates are objects: `===` compares identity, `new Date(date)` makes a new Date, and Dates compile in arrays (`includes`, `indexOf`, `lastIndexOf`, `join`, and default `sort` by `toString()` text), in `Date | undefined` and `Date | null` slots, as Map and Set keys and values, and in records, tuples, class fields, closures, promises and generators.
  Unions of a Date with other non-nullish types are unsupported.
  Zero-argument, single number/string, and local-time year/month field constructors, `getTime`, `valueOf`, `toISOString`, `toUTCString`, `toJSON` (a `string | null`: null for an invalid date, as in Node), `toString`, `toDateString`, `toTimeString`, `String(date)` and template literal conversion, relational comparison, calendar getters, `getTimezoneOffset`, and single-string `Date.parse` compile.
  A Date converted to `unknown` is the same Date object.
  See [Dates and timezones](#dates-and-timezones) for timezone data.
