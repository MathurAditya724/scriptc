- **Compression:** `zlib.deflateSync`, `deflateRawSync`, and `gzipSync` accept default options or an inline `{ level: n }` with an integer literal from `-1` through `9`. Other options and runtime-valued levels are unsupported. The `Deflate`, `Inflate`, `Gzip`, `Gunzip`, `DeflateRaw`, `InflateRaw`, and `Unzip` streams, from `createGzip()` and the other factories or `new zlib.Gzip()`, accept Node's options and support writes, piping, `pipeline`, async iteration, `flush`, `params`, `close`, `reset`, and `bytesWritten`. Each output chunk is its own Buffer rather than a slice of a shared chunk-size buffer. `zlib.constants` members and their module-level copies read as Node's values. `util.inspect`, `instanceof zlib.Gzip`, and Brotli and Zstd compression are unsupported.
