- **Streams and readline:** Stream chunks are Buffers or strings. Object-mode streams of other values, `Readable.from` over arrays of other values, the `Readable` iterator helpers (`map`, `filter`, `toArray`, and the others), `stream.compose`, and generator stages in `pipeline` are unsupported. `pipeline` from `node:stream/promises` accepts a trailing `{ signal, end }` object, and `finished` accepts `readable`, `writable`, `error`, `cleanup`, and `signal` options, with boolean literals for the flags. Stream constructors accept a `signal` option in an options object literal, and `stream.addAbortSignal` accepts any stream. `node:readline` and `node:readline/promises` interfaces read `process.stdin` or a readable stream without terminal mode: `question`, `prompt`, `write`, `pause`, `resume`, `'line'`/`'close'`/`'pause'`/`'resume'`/`'error'` listeners, and `for await` compile. Terminal mode, `completer`, history options, and chained listener registration are unsupported.
