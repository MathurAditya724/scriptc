- **Streams and readline:** Stream chunks are Buffers or strings. `Readable.from` accepts arrays, generators and async generators whose values are strings or Buffers; a stream that mixes strings and Buffers is unsupported. Object-mode streams of other values, `Readable.from` over other iterables, and generator stages in `pipeline` are unsupported. `Readable.from` accepts `objectMode` and `highWaterMark` options. The `Readable` operators (`map`, `filter`, `forEach`, `toArray`, `some`, `find`, `every`, `flatMap`, `drop`, `take`, and `reduce`) accept `concurrency`, `highWaterMark`, and `signal` options; their callbacks take only the chunk, `map` returns strings or Buffers, and `flatMap` returns strings or arrays of them. `readable.compose` is unsupported. `stream.compose` accepts two or more streams, or one `Duplex`; functions, iterables, and web streams as stages are unsupported, and the `ERR_INVALID_ARG_VALUE` message for a stream that is no longer readable or writable names the stream's class instead of printing the stream. `pipeline` from `node:stream/promises` accepts a trailing `{ signal, end }` object, and `finished` accepts `readable`, `writable`, `error`, `cleanup`, and `signal` options, with boolean literals for the flags. Stream constructors accept a `signal` option in an options object literal, and `stream.addAbortSignal` accepts any stream. `node:readline` and `node:readline/promises` interfaces read `process.stdin` or a readable stream without terminal mode: `question`, `prompt`, `write`, `pause`, `resume`, `'line'`/`'close'`/`'pause'`/`'resume'`/`'error'` listeners, and `for await` compile. Terminal mode, `completer`, history options, and chained listener registration are unsupported.
